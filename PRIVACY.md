# Privacy Policy — YouTube Pause

_Last updated: September 1, 2026_

## Summary

YouTube Pause does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Period.

## What the extension does

YouTube Pause shows a brief pause overlay before YouTube videos play, giving you a moment to decide whether you really want to watch. All logic runs locally in your browser.

## Data collection

- **No personal data is collected.** The extension has no analytics, no tracking, and no telemetry.
- **No data leaves your device.** The extension makes no network requests to any server.
- **No third-party services** are used.

## Local storage

The extension uses Chrome's `storage` permission solely to remember your own settings (such as whether blocking is switched on or off). This data is stored locally in your browser, is never transmitted anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

## Permissions

- `storage` — save your on/off preference locally.
- Access to `youtube.com` pages — required to display the pause overlay on YouTube. No page content is read, recorded, or transmitted.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.
